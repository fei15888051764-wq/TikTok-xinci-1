Sleepy girl mocktail is exactly what it sounds like. A drink you make before bed to help you fall asleep, except it's not wine. It's tart cherry juice, magnesium powder, and some kind of fizzy water. TikTok turned it into a whole aesthetic around 2023, and now there are entire accounts dedicated to ranking different versions of it.

The original recipe came from TikToker @caleeshea in January 2023. She posted a video mixing tart cherry juice with prebiotic soda, calling it her sleep aid. A few months later, wellness influencer @gracie_norton added magnesium powder to the mix, claimed it gave her "the best sleep of my life," and the video went viral. Over a million and a half views. By summer 2023, everyone was making it.

## Why it took over

The appeal is that it looks like a cocktail but it's actually just juice and supplements. There's something satisfying about drinking something fancy before bed instead of just taking a melatonin gummy. The "mocktail" framing makes it feel like self-care, not medicine.

TikTokers started adding their own twists. Some use lavender bitters or add Olipop. Others replace the cherry juice with blueberry. The hashtag #SleepyGirlMocktail racked up millions of views, mostly from people filming their nightly routine with soft lighting and calm voiceovers.

The science is mixed. Tart cherry juice does have melatonin. Magnesium can help with relaxation. But the drink isn't magic. It's more of a ritual, the act of making something and drinking it slowly that actually helps you wind down.

## The Real Recipe vs The TikTok Version

| | TikTok Version | Actually Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Cherry juice** | Tart cherry juice (has melatonin) | Any tart cherry juice |
| **Magnesium** | Powder mixed in | Pill form, same effect |
| **Fizzy water** | Olipop or prebiotic soda | Plain soda water |
| **The secret** | The ritual of making it | Actually going to bed on time |

Rule of thumb: if you are still scrolling TikTok at 1am, no amount of cherry juice will save you. Which, fair. We have all been there.
