You say "hear me out" right before confessing something that could end the friendship. It's a request for a fair trial. "Hear me out: Shrek is hot." Buffer, colon, crime. That structure never changes.

In plain English it just means "let me finish before you react." TikTok kept the grammar and raised the stakes.

## The cake that launched a thousand crushes

In September 2024, TikTok made the phrase edible. The "hear me out cake" (aka the smash-or-pass cake): print photos of your questionable crushes, tape them to popsicle sticks, stab them into a real cake, reveal them one at a time like a deranged award show.

By October it was everywhere; I saw my first one at a birthday party that month. Over on Reddit, one explainer summed it up: "It is people they think are hot but it's unconventional/embarrassing." The recurring picks, if you want the full horror: Homer Simpson, Ben Stiller in Night at the Museum, Garfield, Woody, Sirius Black, Fred from Scooby Doo, Mr. Potato Head, Spirit the horse, Mike Wazowski.

The approval script never changes either: "THESE ARE SO VALID THO." "I'M HEARING YALL OUT." You'll find both under every cake video.

Then it escaped parties: the @danielle.guizio office did one as a team builder, one family filmed a round with their little kid. That video got deleted fast. Which, fair.

## What a hear me out looks like

> "hear me out: the grinch. i'm not elaborating"

Captions get hyper-specific: "hear me out: sirius black, prisoner of azkaban specifically." The specificity does all the work.

> @cakejudge47: SPIRIT THE HORSE IS CRAZY... i'm hearing you out tho

Someone at a party pulls Mr. Potato Head out of the cake. The room doesn't even hesitate: "valid. next."

## Not every crush deserves a cake

An Autostraddle writer watched 47 of these videos and reported back: straight people's picks are almost all cartoons, especially animals and monsters. Naming a real human as your embarrassing crush is kind of mean to that human; a cartoon gives safe distance. The picks still leak what you actually value. Loyalty, mostly. Maybe a strong ogre jawline.

Honestly, she's right. The trend only works when it's self-aware. Put a real coworker on the cake and it's an HR ticket, not a bit. The family video deserved the delete.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does hear me out mean on TikTok?

The buffer phrase before an unconventional or embarrassing crush confession. You're asking for patience before the judgment lands.

### How does the hear me out cake work?

Print the picks, tape them to popsicle sticks, plant them in a cake, reveal one at a time. The room declares each one valid or criminal.

### Do people still say hear me out?

The cake peaked in fall 2024, but the phrase survived as a caption format. Nobody's baking anymore. The confessions continue.

## Pronunciation

Say it "HEER me OUT," heavy on the OUT, like you're bracing for impact.
